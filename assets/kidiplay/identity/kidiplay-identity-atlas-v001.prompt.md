# KidiPlay Identity Atlas — v001

> **Statut : rejetée comme spritesheet.** La direction artistique reste une
> exploration utile, mais la composition emploie des cellules de tailles
> variables. Elle ne doit pas servir de grille de production.

## Intention

Première exploration de l’identité visuelle de KidiPlay sous la forme d’une
planche d’assets destinée à devenir une spritesheet découpable.

## Prompt de génération

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: first visual identity atlas and sliceable sprite-sheet concept for
the children's project “KidiPlay”

Primary request: Create one coherent KidiPlay visual identity asset board
containing exactly 1 main mascot, 6 main menu icons, 4 animals, 3 illustrated
buttons, 2 small decorative environment elements, and a visible color palette.

Art direction: gentle contemporary children’s illustration; rounded friendly
shapes; subtle colored-pencil and cut-paper texture; thick clean dark-warm
outlines; joyful readable expressions; polished but handmade; suitable for
young children; warm pastel palette with coral, apricot, butter yellow, mint,
sky blue, lavender, warm cream and soft brown.

Mascot: one cheerful, gender-neutral bear cub mascot with a distinctive small
coral neckerchief, full body, front three-quarter view.

Menu icons: six separate pictorial icons representing play, memory cards,
coloring, simple puzzles, music, and stickers; no letters and no written words.

Animals: four separate friendly full-body animals: rabbit, fox, elephant and
lion cub.

Buttons: three separate rounded illustrated button shapes, one coral, one mint
and one sky blue, each with a simple non-text pictogram and ample empty center
space; no text.

Decor: two separate small scenery assets: a rounded flowering bush and a small
soft cloud with tiny stars.

Composition: clean landscape asset board laid out as a strict regular grid of
spacious rectangular cells. Exactly one isolated asset per cell, clearly
separated with generous consistent padding, no overlaps, no cropped objects.
Add a compact row of eight circular color swatches along the bottom, separate
from all asset cells. Maintain consistent scale within each asset family. This
must read both as an art-direction board and as a future sliceable sprite sheet.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly uniform warm off-white background, flat and
untextured, with no floor plane.

Lighting/mood: soft, cheerful and welcoming; minimal shading; no cast shadows.

Constraints: all requested elements must share one unmistakably consistent
visual language; crisp silhouettes; every element fully visible; no grid
labels; no captions; no typography; no logo; no watermark.

Avoid: realistic rendering, 3D plastic look, gradients in the background,
photographic texture, busy decoration, overlapping cells, extra characters,
extra objects, repeated assets, text, letters, numbers.
```

## Hypothèses de cette exploration

- La mascotte proposée est un ourson au foulard corail.
- Les six usages illustrés sont : jouer, Memory, coloriage, puzzle, musique et
  autocollants.
- Les quatre animaux sont : lapin, renard, éléphant et lionceau.
- Cette version reste une candidate : la planche et chaque cellule doivent être
  validées avant découpage ou déclinaison.
