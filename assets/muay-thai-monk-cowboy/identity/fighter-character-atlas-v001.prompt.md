# Muay Thai Monk-Cowboy — Character Atlas v001

## Brief utilisateur

> Un combattant de Muay Thai très balèze et musclé, entre un moine et un
> cow-boy.

Le nom du projet est descriptif et provisoire.

## Prompt principal

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: uniform character identity atlas and motion-key sprite sheet for a
side-view 2D arcade beat'em all.

Input image: the supplied image is the mandatory 4-by-4 layout scaffold and
edit target.

Primary request: Fill the supplied grid with exactly sixteen full-body
depictions of the same original heavyweight Muay Thai fighter, whose visual
identity sits between a contemplative monk and a frontier cowboy. Preserve the
square canvas, all four columns, all four rows, every straight dark-purple grid
line, and every equal cell exactly. Never merge, resize, bend, erase, round, or
add grid divisions.

Character identity, identical in every cell: enormous extremely muscular adult
man, heavyweight proportions, shaved head, broad calm face, powerful neck and
shoulders. He wears a weathered broad-brim frontier hat strapped flat against
his upper back rather than covering his head; a sleeveless saffron monk robe
crossed over one shoulder and cut like a short practical poncho; dark indigo
Muay Thai shorts with a simple tooled-leather waistband; thick traditional rope
hand wraps in dark red; large wooden meditation beads around one wrist; rugged
brown leather shin and ankle guards subtly recalling cowboy boots but with no
heels and no spurs; bare feet for combat. A turquoise cloth accent and small
brass details unify the design. No firearm, no branding, no writing. Keep face,
body mass, costume, palette, hat, beads, wraps and proportions strictly
consistent in all sixteen cells.

Fixed cell map, left to right:
Row 1: neutral front reference; neutral three-quarter reference; neutral side
profile facing right; neutral back reference.
Row 2: side-view combat idle facing right; high Muay Thai guard; heavy walk
contact pose; explosive forward run pose.
Row 3: straight teep push kick; powerful roundhouse kick; rising knee strike in
clinch posture with no opponent; horizontal elbow strike.
Row 4: low defensive check; readable hit reaction; calm kneeling meditation
pose; quiet victory pose with hands together and the frontier hat now held
against his chest.

Style/medium: energetic hand-drawn 1990s arcade beat'em all character art,
production sprite concept, bold dark ink contours, expressive angular linework,
clean cel shading with two or three shade levels, slightly exaggerated heroic
anatomy, strong readable silhouettes, crisp forms at game scale. Not pixel art,
not 3D, not photorealistic.

Color palette: sun-baked saffron and ochre, dark indigo, dusty turquoise,
oxblood red wraps, warm brown leather, small antique-brass accents, near-black
outlines. Warm western desert tones balanced by monastic restraint.

Composition: one complete isolated full-body depiction centered in each cell;
consistent character height, head size and body scale; approximately 10 percent
empty padding on every side. Limbs, hat, robe and all accessories must remain
fully inside their own cell. All action poses use the same side-view camera and
common ground line. Reference views in row 1 may rotate as specified.

Backdrop: preserve the flat uniform warm-cream cell backgrounds and exact
dark-purple grid. No environment, no floor plane, no cast shadows.

Constraints: exactly sixteen depictions of one character, no alternate
costumes, no enemies, no extra people, no horses, no guns, no weapons, no text,
no labels, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no cropped limbs, no object crossing
any grid line, no duplicated pose, anatomically coherent hands and feet.
```
