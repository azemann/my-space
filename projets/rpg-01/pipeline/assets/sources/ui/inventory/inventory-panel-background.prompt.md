# Prompt — fond du panneau d'inventaire

## Mode

ImageGen intégré, génération bitmap avec la prévisualisation de la Plage du
Réveil comme référence de style et de palette.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: production bitmap background for a Godot RPG inventory panel
Primary request: create one empty rectangular inventory panel background, no interface controls and no text, designed to sit behind a programmable grid of inventory slots.
Input images: Image 1 is a style and palette reference from the game's top-down beach scene; use its warm golden sand, weathered shipwreck wood, muted iron fittings, and small turquoise water accents, but do not reproduce the scene.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted pixel-art RPG UI, crisp at low resolution, restrained detail, coherent with 32px top-down pixel art.
Composition/framing: exact 16:9 rectangular panel, symmetrical border, broad quiet dark interior area with even contrast for 5 columns of buttons, subtle header band at top and subtle footer band at bottom. Keep all essential ornament inside a 24px safe border. Corners and edges should read clearly when scaled.
Color palette: dark desaturated brown leather or wood interior, warm sand-gold trim, weathered brown wood, aged iron, tiny restrained turquoise/cyan accents suggesting sea and psychokinesis.
Materials/textures: light leather grain, worn wood, small rivets, minimal rope motifs, no busy decoration in the central content area.
Constraints: no words, no letters, no numbers, no icons inside slots, no slot grid, no characters, no items, no watermark, no logo, no transparency, no cast shadow outside the panel. The image must be a flat front-facing UI asset with no perspective. Output at 1536x864 or equivalent 16:9 high resolution.
```
